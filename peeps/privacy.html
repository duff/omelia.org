Peeps Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Peeps is a desktop address book for your Google contacts. It runs on your computer. There is no Peeps server, and the developer never receives, sees, or stores your data.

What Peeps accesses

When you connect a Google account, Peeps asks Google for access to your contacts, using the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts. With it, Peeps reads your contacts and their details, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, birthdays, notes, and photos. It adds, changes, and deletes contacts only when you ask it to.

Where your data is kept

Everything Peeps keeps stays on your computer:

  • A copy of your contacts, so search is fast, in ~/.local/share/peeps
  • Contact photos in ~/.cache/peeps
  • Settings in ~/.config/peeps
  • The Google sign-in token in your computer's login keyring
  • vCards made when you share a contact, in a temporary folder that is cleared when you log out

Who Peeps talks to

Peeps connects only to Google: to sign you in, and to read and save your contacts and photos through the Google People API. It has no analytics, no tracking, and no ads. When you choose to share a contact, by email or with LocalSend, that contact goes where you send it.

Sharing and selling

Peeps does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. Your data is handled only by Peeps on your computer and by Google. It is never used for advertising or to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Peeps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

In Peeps, Menu → Google account → Disconnect removes the sign-in and the copy of your contacts from your computer. To remove everything, also delete the folders listed above. To revoke Peeps' access on Google's side, visit your Google Account's third-party connections. Your contacts themselves stay in your Google account.

Changes
