Peeps

A keyboard-first address book for Omarchy.

Peeps keeps your Google contacts on your Linux desktop. Search answers from a copy on your computer, so finding someone does not wait on the network. From the keyboard you can copy a phone number or address, start an email, share a contact as a vCard, and add or edit people. The window follows your Omarchy theme and text size. A command line lists, searches, exports, and changes the same contacts.

Why Peeps asks for your Google contacts

Your contacts live in your Google account, so they stay the same on your phone and everywhere else you use Google. When you connect, Peeps asks Google for permission to see, edit, download, and delete your contacts. It needs that to show your contacts and to save the changes you make. It only adds, changes, or deletes a contact when you do.

Peeps runs entirely on your computer. Your contacts travel between your computer and Google, and nowhere else. The privacy policy has the details.
